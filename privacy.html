Privacy Policy

How Activities handles data in this MVP.

This policy explains how information is collected, used and stored for the Activities website. Please review it with legal counsel before a production launch.

Last updated:

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide through forms, such as your name, email address, phone number, provider details and any message you choose to send.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, evaluate partner applications, improve platform discovery and support future booking or reminder functionality.

3. Local reminder storage

Saved reminders in this MVP are stored locally in your browser so your shortlist remains visible across pages. They are not synced to a server in this front-end demo.

4. Sharing of data

We do not intend to sell personal information. Data may be shared with service providers, support tools or partner operations only where needed to deliver the service.

5. Retention and security

We aim to retain data only as long as necessary for support, legal compliance and product operations, while using reasonable safeguards appropriate to platform maturity.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@activitieshub.in.
