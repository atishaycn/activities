T&C for partners

Partner terms for providers, schools, studios and workshop hosts.

These terms describe the expectations that apply when a partner submits or maintains listings on the Activities platform.

Last updated:

1. Accuracy of listings

Partners must provide accurate details for age-group, pricing, schedules, locations, program descriptions and enrollment rules.

2. Right to review

Activities may review, edit, reject or remove listings that do not meet platform quality, safety or positioning standards.

3. Compliance and permissions

Partners are responsible for obtaining any licenses, permissions, staff clearances and insurance needed for their services.

4. Fees and future monetization

Commercial terms for featured placements, subscriptions, lead fees or transactions may be introduced in a separate agreement.

5. Customer handling

Partners remain responsible for delivery quality, attendance policies, refunds, conduct and direct communication with enrolled families.

6. Contact

For partner questions, contact partners@activitieshub.in.
