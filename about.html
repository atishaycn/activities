About Activities

A discovery platform designed around how parents actually decide.

Activities helps families discover high-fit classes and workshops, align them with real schedules, and remember what matters. The MVP focuses on the most practical parent jobs: finding options, planning around the calendar, and following through.

Platform offering

What families get

  • Curated discovery across extra-curricular, sports, academic and camp categories.
  • Filters for area, age-group and month to support faster shortlisting.
  • Saved reminders and downloadable calendar events to reduce planning drop-off.
  • Partner-friendly listing flow for studios, schools and workshop hosts.
Our mission

Turn fragmented browsing into confident family planning.

Parents often jump between Instagram pages, school WhatsApp groups, neighborhood recommendations and provider websites. Activities reduces that fragmentation by creating a single discovery layer with enough structure to support better decisions.

Instead of overwhelming parents with endless options, the product steers them toward a manageable shortlist and helps them remember what they intended to try.

Founder story

Built from a familiar parent pain point.

The founder perspective behind Activities is simple: modern families have more activity choices than ever, but less time and attention to compare them well. Finding the right class is hard enough; remembering trial dates, balancing school schedules and avoiding over-commitment is even harder.

That insight shaped a practical MVP — one that respects busy parents, supports trusted local providers and creates a cleaner bridge between discovery and enrollment.

  • Founder lens: parent-first, planning-led and trust-oriented.
  • Problem observed: discovery is fragmented across WhatsApp, Instagram and word of mouth.
  • What the founder is building: a platform that helps parents discover, shortlist and remember the right activities.
How the product works

Three focused jobs-to-be-done

This release keeps the website deliberately narrow so the user journey stays clear and the value proposition is immediately understandable.

  1. Discovery first. Parents browse by category and filter by area, age-group and month to surface relevant options quickly.
  2. Calendar alignment next. Each listing exposes schedule information and a downloadable calendar action so the family can check fit before committing.
  3. Reminders to close the loop. Parents save reminders for classes and reads so promising options do not get lost after the initial browse.

What makes this different

Positioning for an early-stage launch

The first version does not try to be everything at once. It focuses on building trust at the discovery layer and creating habits around planning.

Parent-first curation

Category structure reflects how parents think: extra-curriculars, sports, academics, enrichment, camps, workshops and trend-based inspiration.

Operationally lightweight MVP

Even without a complex booking stack, the site still creates value through navigation, filtering, reminders, partner acquisition and calendar support.

Clear path to monetization

Long-term extensions can include featured placements, subscription benefits, booking fees, credits, concierge planning and partner SaaS tools.
